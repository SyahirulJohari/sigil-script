Arcane Evolution · Spellcraft Balance

Read enemy weaknesses

❄ Frost → ♨ Ember — Thermal Shatter. Bonus damage, +20 score.

♨ Ember → ❄ Frost — Steam Burst. Bonus damage, +20 score.

♨/❄ → ✦ Arcane — Arcane Discharge consumes Burn or Chill for a finishing burst.

◇ Ward → attack — Absorb a hit to empower your next attack by 30% (6 seconds).

↻ Repeat ×2 — Two rapid pulses, second at 72% power; ✺ Burst — 60% splash to nearby enemies.

♨ Cinder Imp — Resists Fire; vulnerable to Frost.

❄ Mist Wisp — Resists Frost; vulnerable to Fire.

▣ Sentinel — Resists standard attacks; Pierce breaks armour.

✦ Warden — Its weakness changes throughout the fight.

⚔ Hunter AI — Enemies chase you, but now clearly warn you before attacking. Escape the marked area or Dash to avoid damage.

⚠ Attack warnings — Orange cones = Imp lunges; cyan lines = Wisp shots; amber circles = Sentinel slams; purple sigils / rays = Warden abilities.

☾ Warden patterns — Arc Volley, Sigil Burst and Rift Nova rotate during the boss fight. Reducing the Warden below half health makes its attacks more frequent.

⌁ If Afflicted + ✺ Catalyst — Build powerful custom reaction spells.
